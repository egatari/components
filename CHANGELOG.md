## 1.13.0 - 2026-10-27

### Added

- `m-heatmap` (series academic 数理・統計, purposes `compare`, `correlation`; paid packs only, not in the free pack for now): a grid of cells coloured by value, for confusion matrices, correlation matrices and attention maps. Child JSON `{"rows": [labels], "cols": [labels], "values": [[…]], "unit"?, "digits"?, "highlight"?: [[row, col] or [row, col, step]]}` (0-based; a row or column count that does not match the labels is an error and shows the placeholder). Colours are theme colours only, mixed with `color-mix(in oklab, …, var(--bg))`: `scale="sequential"` (default) from `--bg` to `tone` (`term` default, `emph`, `accent2`), `scale="diverging"` around `center` (default 0) with `tone` above and `lowtone` (`emph` default) below; `range="[lo,hi]"` fixes the scale (otherwise [min(0, smallest), largest], or symmetric around the centre). Cell colours follow the scale exactly (the colour is the data). Each cell's number is drawn in `--fg` or `--bg`, whichever has the higher computed contrast against that cell; where even that is under 4.5:1 (mid lightness) the number gets a 3 px halo in the other colour (`paint-order: stroke`). A legend bar on the right (min, centre, max). `normalize="row"` (proportions per row, confusion matrix; numbers `show="percent" | "count" | "both"`), `diag="on"` (diagonal outlined in `--fg`), highlights outlined in `--emph` (both over a `--bg` halo), `numbers="auto" | "on" | "off"`. Steps: `build="rows"` (one row per step; the default with `normalize="row"`), `"whole"`, `"cells"` (the grid, then one highlight per step; the default with highlights). Labels at 26 px or 24 px, never smaller: long column labels turn vertical (`rotate="auto" | "0" | "90"`), and when there are too many only every k-th label is shown, with a warning; above 12×12 the cell numbers turn off automatically, with a warning, and they turn off whenever they would need less than 24 px. Row labels, column labels, the legend and the empty grid show from step 0; under `ctx.preview` later cells and outlines at the theme's `--preview-opacity`. Readable layer: a `<table>` of the values (the standard visually-hidden pattern: absolute, 1 px, clip, overflow hidden; highlighted cells marked); the drawing is aria-hidden. Variants `fade` (0) and `sweep` (cells of a step from the top left, within the same step length)
- `m-map-world` (series social 社会, purpose `spatial`; not in the free pack for now): the countries and regions of the world, the companion of `m-map-japan` (docs/map-design.md). Equal Earth, central meridian 150°E (Pacific-centred only, map-design §6 2; the seam is at 30°W and a flow whose short way crosses it warns). Child JSON as in `m-map-japan`: `regions` (`id` = ISO alpha-3 / Natural Earth code / Japanese name, or `group` = the six regions 六州 from Natural Earth's `REGION_UN`; overseas parts are coloured with the region of their geography: the overseas departments and territories フランス領ギアナ → 南アメリカ州 and レユニオン → アフリカ州, while parts of a country's own territory stay with it: ハワイ州 and アラスカ州 with the United States in 北アメリカ州, カナリア諸島 (an autonomous community) with Spain in ヨーロッパ州; Antarctica is in no region, grey, and named 南極大陸 with `labels="all"`), `categories` (sets of countries with a legend, up to 4 series colours), `values` + `classes` (choropleth with a legend; classes as in `m-map-japan`), `points`, `flows`, `shapes`; `grid="equator|graticule"`; variants `fade` `sweep` `draw`; readable layer

## 1.12.0 - 2026-10-26

### Added

- `m-syntax` (series english 英語, purpose `text-structure`; not in the free pack for now): English sentence structure for 中学・高校 English lessons, the English counterpart of `m-kobun` / `m-gendai`. Short marked-up text inside the element, one sentence per line, `= …` the Japanese translation of the sentence above. `[S …] [V …] [O …] [C …] [M …]` (also `O1` `O2`) mark the sentence elements: an underline under the words and the letter under it (28 px; colours `--series-1..4`, M `--muted`, the letter always carries the role); elements inside a bracket show in lower case (s v o c m) one row above, so the sentence's S V O C line up on one row. `{形 …}` `{副 …}` `{名 …}` mark phrases and clauses, drawn as ( ) < > [ ] (shape and name both carry the kind) with the name (形容詞句, 副詞節, …, 24 px) above the opening bracket; 句 or 節 is decided by a `[V …]` inside, or written (`{形節 …}`). Modifier arrows (`--emph`): `{形^ …}` to the word before the bracket, `tall^` to the next word, `{副^plays …}` to the nearest `plays` outside the bracket; overlapping arrows take separate lanes, arrows to one word are spread over its width. `boy[名]` parts of speech (`名 代 動 助 形 副 冠 前 接 関 間`), shown under the words with `pos="on"`. Steps: (parts of speech) → each element present, in the order S V O C M (shared by all sentences) → brackets (with the lower-case elements inside) → arrows → translation; `build="whole"` puts them all in one step, one after another; `yaku="false"` drops the translation step. Words are HTML in `var(--font-sans)` (36 px by default, never below 27; translation 28 px) and wrap between words; the space for brackets is kept from step 0 so the sentence never moves, underlines are cut per line and brackets stay beside their words across a wrap. Writing errors (unclosed or crossed brackets, unknown letters, kinds or parts of speech, an arrow head not found, a translation before any sentence), an arrow across lines and a sentence taller than the element are reported with `console.warn`. Lecture view: later steps at the theme's `--preview-opacity`. Readable layer (sr-only): sentence, elements, brackets, arrows, parts of speech, translation. Variants `fade` (0) and `draw` (underlines, brackets and arrows drawn as lines). README pack-visible 941 bytes (`tools/pack-bytes.mjs`)

### Added

- `m-keyboard` (series music 音楽, purpose `music`; not in the free pack for now): a piano keyboard for school music lessons. Child JSON `{"range": "C4-C6", "names": "solfa" | "abc" | "iroha" | "none", "cumulative": bool, "dur": s, "steps": [{"keys": ["C4", "E4", "G4"], "label", "t": [s per key], "tone", "dur"}]}`. `range` covers 1–3 octaves (12–36 semitones) and starts and ends on white keys; the part draws its own SVG keyboard: white keys as rectangles, black keys 0.58 wide and 0.62 long, on top, centred as on a real keyboard (the C–E group split into fifths and the F–B group into sevenths: C♯/D♯ and F♯/A♯ lean outwards, G♯ is centred). White-key names under the keyboard (ドレミファソラシ, CDEFGAB or ハニホヘトイロ); black-key names (ド♯ / C♯ / 嬰ハ, レ♭ / D♭ / 変ニ) only on lit keys, above the keyboard (a second row when two would touch). Each step lights its keys in `--emph` (or the step's `tone`) with a filled dot; a lit black key keeps its dark rim around the coloured inside; `cumulative: true` keeps earlier steps' keys lit, faded, with a ring instead of the dot (never colour alone). Per-key `t` staggers a scale or arpeggio, equal `t` is a chord. `events(k)` (spec §8.4 "Component sounds"): one `note` per key, `pitch` the key name, `vel` 0.7, `dur` from `dur` (step, then top level) or else to the next later `t` (0.8 s for the last); `duration(k)` reaches the end of the last note. Sound is never the only carrier: the lit keys show the same. Step labels 30 px above the keyboard. The lecture view (`ctx.preview`, or `ctx.mode === "lecture"` from older players) shows later steps' keys faint without the dot, at the theme's `--preview-opacity` read with getComputedStyle (spec §8.4 "Answers under preview"; 0.45 only when the variable is missing). The part has no controls. Variants `light` (0, the light fades in), `press` (the key dips 4 px and comes back), `rise` (the light fills the key from the bottom), all within 0.3 s and within the note. Range, key-name and `t`-length errors show the placeholder
