# m-checklist changelog

The part's own versions (Egatari spec §6.1). Releases of the whole library are in the repository's CHANGELOG.md.

## 2.0.0 (components 2.9.0)

- New part: a checklist. Items are listed (or, with `reveal`, appear by step) and one mark is drawn per step: ✓ (`--term`), × (`--emph`) or none. The marks are the author's slide content; nothing a viewer does is stored.
